## Formula checks
- General birth–death local balance: π_n λ_n=π_{n+1} μ_{n+1}.
- M/M/1 stability: ρ=λ/μ<1.
- Stationary law: π_n=(1−ρ)ρ^n.
- L=ρ/(1−ρ); Lq=ρ²/(1−ρ).
- W=1/(μ−λ); Wq=ρ/(μ−λ).
- Little’s law: L=λW and Lq=λWq.
