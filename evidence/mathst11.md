# Evidence map — Mathematics T11

| Claim | Exact anchor |
|---|---|
| Stationary points | Solve fₓ=fᵧ=0 |
| Hessian determinant | D=fₓₓfᵧᵧ−fₓᵧ² |
| Saddle | x²−y² at origin, D=−4 |
| Local minimum | x²+2y² at origin, D=8 and fₓₓ>0 |
| Line constraint | Minimise x²+y² subject to x+y=1; value 1/2 |
| Circle constraint | Extrema of x+y on x²+y²=1 are ±√2 |

References: OpenStax Calculus Volume 3; MIT OCW 18.02SC; Apostol, *Mathematical Analysis*.
