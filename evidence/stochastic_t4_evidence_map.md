# Evidence map — Stochastic T4: Classification of States

| Lesson claim / skill | Primary evidence | Validation approach |
|---|---|---|
| Communication is mutual reachability and partitions the state space | Norris, *Markov Chains* | Directed-graph reachability |
| A communicating class is closed when no transition leaves it | Norris; Ross, *Introduction to Probability Models* | Inspect all outgoing edges |
| In a finite chain, closed communicating classes are recurrent and states outside them are transient | Ross; Norris | Identify closed classes in supplied finite graph |
| Period d(i)=gcd{n≥1:p_ii^(n)>0} | Norris | Compute gcd of possible return times |
| Communicating states share the same period | Norris | Standard communicating-class theorem |
| Absorbing states have period one; alternating two-state chain has period two | Direct return-time calculation | Explicit return-time sets |
