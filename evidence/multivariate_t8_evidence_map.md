## Scope controls
- Mahalanobis distance: inverse-covariance quadratic form; known-parameter normal chi-square reference.
- LDA versus QDA: common versus group-specific covariance assumptions.
- MANOVA: joint equality of mean response vectors; Wilks, Pillai, Hotelling–Lawley and Roy criteria.
- Excludes detailed finite-sample derivations, classification error estimation and advanced MANOVA transformations.
