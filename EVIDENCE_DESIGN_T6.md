# Evidence map — Unit III, Design of Experiments, Topic 3B.6

- Topic: design-t6, Confounding, split-plot designs and PBIBD.
- No official PYQ is claimed; mastery items are original practice.
- Confounding: selected factorial contrast is aliased with block contrasts; complete confounding repeats the same effect across replications; partial confounding rotates effects.
- Split plot: hard-to-change factor is assigned to whole plots; easy-to-change factor is randomised within subplots. The whole-plot factor uses whole-plot error; subplot factor and interaction use subplot error in the standard balanced model.
- PBIBD: pairs are classified into associate classes; λ_i is concurrence for i-th associates. BIBD is the special uniform-pair-concurrence case.
- The lesson states the randomisation hierarchy and avoids treating subplots as independent whole-plot replicates.
- verify_design_t6.py checks key block counts, split-plot error-stratum statements and PBIBD class-concurrence assertions.
